Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanskr

SIL Open Font License 1.1; full text: assets/fonts/OFL.txt (FONT_LICENSE.txt in the Windows build).
Font is bundled for Korean UI and is not downloaded at runtime.

Character direction reference
art/references/character-directions.png is an AI-generated concept comparison
created for this project. Its old direction C was superseded on 2026-10-07 by
art/references/mature-rpg-2026-10-07/a-stylized-adventure.png. These AI concept
references are excluded from the game build and are not actual game models.

The expedition_adult.glb, expedition_axe.glb and carpenter_hammer.glb meshes,
skin and animation clips are originally authored in
art/source/characters/build_adventurer.py. They use no downloaded source model.
The environment profiles and surface shaders are also authored for this project.
CampArt's workshop, kitchen, supply cart, wagon hardware and cargo, animal packs,
and the texture-free camp fire shader are original procedural assets in
scripts/visuals/camp_art.gd and assets/shaders/camp_fire.gdshader.
The forest cedar/fern meshes and ruin gateway, masonry, paving, roots, record stone
and minerals are original procedural assets in scripts/visuals/forest_art.gd and
scripts/visuals/ruin_art.gd. The forest_foliage and ruin_stone shaders are original
and use no external texture or model source.
All current geometry, UI layout and attack effects are authored for this project.
No external character model, texture pack,
music or game franchise assets are included.
